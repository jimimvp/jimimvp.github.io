About

Hi! I am a Member of Technical Staff at Prometheus.

My research interests revolve around principled methods for discovery and self-improvement — creating intelligence that can continually adapt and become more efficient in learning and extracting new knowledge. To this end, I have gained rich experience across several sub-areas of machine learning including reinforcement learning and large generative models, leading efforts in both academic and industrial settings. Some of my recent efforts are centered on post-training for agentic LLMs and distributional optimization in diffusion and flow-matching models.

Academic Background

Academically, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the ETH AI Center in Zurich, under the supervision of Andreas Krause, Marco Hutter, and Bernhard Schölkopf, where I also continue to co-supervise students as a Guest Scientist.

I completed my PhD degree summa cum laude (top 1–5%) at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Tübingen, Germany as part of the International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems (IMPRS-IS). My thesis, Structured, Constrained and Creative Learning, was examined by Georg Martius, Peter Dayan, Mathias Niepert, and Matthias Hein, with my advisory committee consisting of Georg Martius, Peter Dayan, and Michael Muehlebach.

Prior to my doctorate, I earned an MSc in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning focus) from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany, and a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Zagreb, Croatia.
