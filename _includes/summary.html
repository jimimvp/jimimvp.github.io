I am a Member of Technical Staff at Prometheus. My research focuses on principled methods for discovery and self-improvement — building intelligence that continually adapts, explores, and extracts new knowledge across reinforcement learning, large generative models, and post-training for agentic LLMs.

Previously Research Scientist at Meta Superintelligence Labs, postdoc at the ETH AI Center (with Andreas Krause, Marco Hutter & Bernhard Schölkopf), and PhD summa cum laude at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (with Georg Martius & Peter Dayan), with research stints at DeepMind, Meta GenAI, and Amazon.

Full academic biography & background

Academically, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the ETH AI Center, under the supervision of Andreas Krause, Marco Hutter and Bernhard Schölkopf. I completed my PhD degree summa cum laude, examined by Georg Martius, Peter Dayan, Mathias Niepert and Matthias Hein, at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Tübingen, Germany as part of the International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems. My thesis advisory committee consisted of Georg Martius, Peter Dayan, and Michael Muehlebach, with my primary supervisor being Georg Martius. In addition, I hold a master's degree in computer science with a machine learning focus from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany, and a bachelor's degree in computer science from the University of Zagreb, Croatia.

In 2017 I received a research fellowship with the Blue Brain Project in Geneva, Switzerland, where I worked at the intersection of neuroscience and reinforcement learning. In 2021 I was an applied scientist intern at Amazon working on variational inference, reinforcement learning and NLP in collaboration with Patrick Ernst and Gyuri Szarvas. In 2022 I spent time at DeepMind, hosted by Kimberly Stachenfeld in Peter Battaglia's group, working on diffusion generative modelling for inverse design in close collaboration with Arnaud Doucet. In 2024 I visited Meta GenAI Zurich to work on efficient large-scale diffusion generative models, before joining Meta Superintelligence Labs as a research scientist working on post-training reasoning and self-improvement.

In my free time I enjoy playing guitar, reading opinionated books on various topics, and all sorts of sports activities. (Read more on the About page →)
